AgriHisaab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026

AgriHisaab is an offline farm-ledger application. Ledger entries, farm and crop details, profile details, and app-lock settings are stored locally on the user's device.

Data collection and sharing

AgriHisaab does not send personal or financial data to the developer, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share user data. The app requests no dangerous Android permissions.

Local storage

The app does not offer backup or restore. Android cloud and device-transfer backup are disabled. Clearing app data, deleting the account, or uninstalling the app may permanently remove the local ledger.

External services

The optional WhatsApp contact action opens an external WhatsApp or browser application. Any information the user chooses to send there is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy terms. AgriHisaab does not send a message automatically.

Data deletion

The user can delete local account data from Profile & Settings or remove the app from the device.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the WhatsApp contact shown inside AgriHisaab: +923013164904.
